Silero Armenian-accent voice notice
=====================================

Bundled model:
- Silero v5 CIS Base (v5_cis_base.pt)
- Speaker: ru_zara (Russian text, Armenian speaker identity)

Official model source:
https://models.silero.ai/models/tts/ru/v5_cis_base.pt
Upstream project:
https://github.com/snakers4/silero-models

The Silero v5 CIS Base models are published under the MIT License.
The Russian stress/accentuation helper is silero-stress 1.5, also published
under the MIT License:
https://github.com/snakers4/silero-stress

This preset uses the ru_zara speaker variant for Russian-language input.
It is a separate Silero CIS model package, while the existing Russian voices
continue to use the existing v5_6_ru.pt model.
