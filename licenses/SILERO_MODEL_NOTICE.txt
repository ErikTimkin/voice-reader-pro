This project uses three speaker IDs from the same local model:
- aidar: ordinary male voice
- eugene: deeper male voice, selected for the "Pudge-style" preset
- xenia: friendly female voice

The "Pudge-style" preset is a generic deep male voice, not an exact voice clone
of the Dota 2 character or its voice actor.
